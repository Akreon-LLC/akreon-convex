# Confidentiality and Non-Disclosure Agreement (Template)

> Draft template for discussion with counsel. Not legal advice.

**Effective Date:** [DATE]

This Confidentiality and Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by and between:

- **Disclosing Party:** Akreon LLC, a [STATE] limited liability company, with principal place of business at [ADDRESS] ("Akreon"); and
- **Receiving Party:** [LEGAL ENTITY NAME], a [ENTITY TYPE/JURISDICTION], with principal place of business at [ADDRESS] ("Recipient").

Akreon and Recipient may be referred to individually as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties."

## 1. Purpose
The Parties wish to discuss and evaluate a potential business relationship regarding Akreon's proprietary software, SDK, and related technology (the "Purpose").

## 2. Definition of Confidential Information
"Confidential Information" means all non-public information disclosed by or on behalf of Akreon, in any form, including source code, object code, APIs, specifications, architecture, models, algorithms, designs, documentation, security details, product plans, pricing, and business information.

Confidential Information includes information disclosed before or after the Effective Date and any notes, analyses, compilations, or derivatives prepared by Recipient that contain or reflect such information.

## 3. Exclusions
Confidential Information does not include information that Recipient can demonstrate by written records:
1. is or becomes publicly available without breach of this Agreement;
2. was lawfully known to Recipient before disclosure by Akreon;
3. is independently developed by Recipient without use of or reference to Akreon's Confidential Information; or
4. is lawfully obtained from a third party without confidentiality restrictions.

## 4. Recipient Obligations
Recipient shall:
1. use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose;
2. protect Confidential Information using at least reasonable care, and no less than the care used for its own highly sensitive information;
3. disclose Confidential Information only to employees, contractors, and advisors with a strict need to know for the Purpose and who are bound by written obligations at least as protective as this Agreement;
4. not reverse engineer, decompile, disassemble, or otherwise attempt to derive non-public aspects of Akreon's technology, except to the extent expressly required by non-waivable law;
5. promptly notify Akreon of any actual or suspected unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.

Recipient is responsible for breaches by its representatives.

## 5. Compelled Disclosure
If Recipient is required by law, court order, or regulation to disclose Confidential Information, Recipient shall (to the extent legally permitted) provide prompt written notice to Akreon and cooperate in seeking protective treatment.

## 6. No License; Ownership
All Confidential Information remains Akreon's property. No license or rights are granted by disclosure, except the limited right to evaluate solely for the Purpose.

## 7. Term and Survival
This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and remains in effect for [3] years.

Recipient's confidentiality and use restrictions for trade secrets survive for so long as the information remains a trade secret under applicable law, and for all other Confidential Information survive for [5] years after termination.

## 8. Return/Destruction
Upon Akreon's request or termination of discussions, Recipient shall promptly return or destroy Confidential Information and certify destruction in writing, except for routine archival backups retained under standard security controls.

## 9. Remedies
Recipient acknowledges unauthorized disclosure or use may cause irreparable harm. Akreon may seek injunctive and equitable relief, in addition to other legal remedies.

## 10. Disclaimer
Confidential Information is provided "AS IS" without warranties of any kind.

## 11. Governing Law; Venue
This Agreement is governed by the laws of [STATE], excluding conflict-of-law rules. The Parties consent to exclusive jurisdiction and venue in [COUNTY/STATE], except that Akreon may seek injunctive relief in any competent jurisdiction.

## 12. Miscellaneous
1. Entire Agreement: This Agreement is the complete agreement regarding confidentiality for the Purpose.
2. Amendments: Any amendment must be in writing and signed by both Parties.
3. Assignment: Recipient may not assign this Agreement without Akreon's prior written consent.
4. Counterparts and eSignatures: Signatures may be in counterparts and electronic form.
