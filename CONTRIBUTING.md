# Contributing

This project is not open source.

## External contributions
Akreon may accept external contributions only after a signed contributor
agreement (CLA or equivalent) that grants Akreon sufficient rights to use,
modify, sublicense, and commercialize contributions.

If you are interested in contributing, contact: david@akreon.ai

## By submitting code, documentation, or other materials
You represent that you have the legal right to submit the contribution and that
submission is subject to Akreon's written contributor agreement terms.

Pull requests without prior written approval may be declined without review.
